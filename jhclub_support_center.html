Withdrawals can be initiated via your Dashboard under the 'Wallet' tab. Ensure your identity is verified (KYC level 2) to prevent processing delays. Most withdrawals are finalized within 15 minutes for crypto or 24 hours for traditional banking.

Lag is often caused by high server distance or local network congestion. We recommend using a wired ethernet connection and checking our 'Server Status' page to ensure your region is operating at 100% capacity.

Pro membership offers reduced tournament fees, priority support access, and early entry into seasonal major events. You can upgrade via the Rewards page using JHC tokens or direct payment.

Security is paramount. If you've lost your 2FA device, please use the 'Account Recovery' form and be prepared to provide proof of identity and recent transaction history. Our security team reviews these manually.
